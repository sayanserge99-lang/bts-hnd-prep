2. Data we collect

  • Account: first name, last name, email address, password (stored hashed, never readable), language, registration date; phone number only if you provide it.
  • Study profile: target exam (BTS or HND), specialty, institution and planned session year if you give them.
  • Consents: purpose (terms of use, this policy, sharing with your institution, terms of sale), version, date given and date withdrawn.
  • Practice activity: saved papers, answers and marks for quizzes and mock exams, progress statistics.
  • Contributions: papers and marking guides you upload, with the date you certified your right to share them; forum posts and reports.
  • Payments (once open): offer, amount, operator (Orange Money or MTN MoMo), phone number used, reference and status. Your Mobile Money PIN is never seen by the site.
  • Security: IP address, used to limit abuse (login attempts, mass sign-ups); log of blocked logins and of access by the administration team.

Visitors without an account give no identity data: only a counter, kept in the browser session, limits the number of papers opened per day. Paper views are counted anonymously.

3. What stays on your phone or computer

  • Cookies: session cookie (to keep you signed in), security cookie against forged forms (CSRF) and language cookie. No advertising or third-party analytics cookies.
  • Local copy of mock exam answers: during a mock exam, answers that have not yet reached the server (for example during a network outage) are kept in the browser (IndexedDB, or localStorage if unavailable). This copy contains only the exam attempt identifier, your answers and the time you entered them. It is deleted as soon as the server confirms receipt, after you hand in your script, and when you sign out (for shared phones). If browser storage is unavailable (private browsing), the exam works without it.
  • Offline mode: if you install the app, saved papers and downloaded quizzes are kept in the browser so you can use them without a connection. They are deleted when a paper is withdrawn or when you remove them.

4. Why we use this data

  • To provide the service: account, past papers, practice, progress tracking, forum (performance of the terms of use).
  • To share your results with your institution, only if you have agreed in your profile; sharing is off by default and can be withdrawn at any time.
  • To collect payments and send you a receipt (performance of the sale, accounting obligations).
  • To protect the site and its users (security, prevention of abuse).
  • To watermark downloaded Premium marking guides with your account identifier, to discourage sharing.

Your data is never sold or used for advertising.
